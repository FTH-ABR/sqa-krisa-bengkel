Konsep mendalam / SQA concept deep dive (15 slaid)

Slaid tambahan dalam deck modul ini yang menerangkan konsep, istilah, piawaian, jenis semakan dan ujian, pengukuran, kawalan dan dokumentasi kualiti di luar KRISA. Slaid bertanda DEEP DIVE.

  1. Three methods of software quality control
  2. SQE process audits vs independent audits
  3. From audit finding to closure
  4. Quality records: evidence, not paperwork
  5. Documentation hierarchy: why documents still matter
  6. Formal design reviews create baselines
  7. Each level builds on the one below. Organisations were appraised against the key process areas of a level, and skipping levels was not allowed.
  8. CMM V1.1 key process areas by level
  9. Mapping CMM key process areas to CMMI process areas
  10. CMMI V1.x architecture
  11. CMMI today: V2.0 (2018) and V3.0 (2023)
  12. Level 4 brings a process under control inside known limits. Level 5 changes the process so that it runs at a new and better capability.
  13. Debate: does process maturity equal capability?
  14. ISO 9001 audit targets for software projects
  15. Review checklist: final acceptance
