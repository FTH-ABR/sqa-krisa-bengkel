Konsep mendalam / SQA concept deep dive (15 slaid)

Slaid tambahan dalam deck modul ini yang menerangkan konsep, istilah, piawaian, jenis semakan dan ujian, pengukuran, kawalan dan dokumentasi kualiti di luar KRISA. Slaid bertanda DEEP DIVE.

  1. Why standards matter
  2. Process standards flow from ISO and CMMI through the organisation manual into project plans. Product standards come from ISO, industry, COTS and the agency.
  3. Process standards vs product standards
  4. ISO 9001:1994: 20 elements as a software quality checklist
  5. ISO 9001:2015: the current clause structure
  6. Standards for SQA planning: then and now
  7. One quality model, ISO 9001 applied through ISO/IEC/IEEE 90003, drives one organisation quality manual, which each project tailors into its own quality plan.
  8. What a Software Quality Plan contains
  9. Independence and continuity of SQA
  10. Lifecycle deliverables vocabulary and KRISA equivalents
  11. Review gates as quality gates
  12. Review checklist: systems engineering
  13. Review checklist: software project planning
  14. Review checklist: software requirements analysis
  15. Baseline acceptance criteria
