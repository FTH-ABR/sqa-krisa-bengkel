Konsep mendalam / SQA concept deep dive (17 slaid)

Slaid tambahan dalam deck modul ini yang menerangkan konsep, istilah, piawaian, jenis semakan dan ujian, pengukuran, kawalan dan dokumentasi kualiti di luar KRISA. Slaid bertanda DEEP DIVE.

  1. Why we review: purpose and intents
  2. Relative cost to fix grows with every phase a defect survives: 1x in requirements, up to 40 to 1000x in maintenance (Pressman, illustrative ratios)
  3. Each step receives errors, passes some through, amplifies others and adds new ones; only its detection efficiency removes them (Pressman model)
  4. Defect amplification, step by step
  5. Formal Technical Review (FTR)
  6. Desk check, peer review, walkthrough, technical review and inspection differ in preparation, roles, records and rigour
  7. Comparing review types by formality
  8. The review meeting: constraints and roles
  9. Reviewer's preparation
  10. Guidelines for a good FTR
  11. Review outcomes and records
  12. Planning, overview, preparation, inspection meeting, rework and follow-up, driven by a checklist of likely errors (Fagan, IBM, 1970s)
  13. Structured walkthroughs and egoless programming
  14. Static analysis techniques
  15. Checklist: preliminary design review
  16. Checklist: design walkthrough
  17. Checklist: coding review
